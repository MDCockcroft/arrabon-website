Draft Battle

How to play

  1. Pick a category. A pool of items appears. Each player has $20 and needs 5 picks.
  2. An item is revealed. The game draws a random item from the category. Nobody chooses what comes up.
  3. Bid back and forth. Players alternate who bids first. Open with a bid, or pass. The other player raises by $1 or more, or passes. The last bid standing wins the item at that price. If both pass, the item is skipped.
  4. You can't go broke. You must always keep at least $1 for every pick you still need. So with 5 picks to go your first bid can never exceed $16.
  5. When both rosters are full, compare drafts and crown a winner. Wins are tracked on this device.

Once a player has all 5 picks, they're out of the bidding and the other player can take or skip each item that comes up for $1.
