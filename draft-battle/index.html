Draft Battle

How to play

  1. Pick a category. A pool of items appears. Each player has $20 and needs 5 picks.
  2. Take turns nominating. The nominating player chooses an item from the pool (or types a wildcard) and sets an opening bid.
  3. Bid back and forth. The other player can raise by $1 or more, or pass. It keeps going until someone passes. The last bid wins the item at that price.
  4. You can't go broke. You must always keep at least $1 for every pick you still need. So with 5 picks to go your first bid can never exceed $16.
  5. When both rosters are full, compare drafts and crown a winner. Wins are tracked on this device.

Once a player has all 5 picks, they're out of the bidding and the other player buys remaining picks at their opening bid.
